# SwedPrime CRM Features Planning Document

## 📋 **Executive Summary**

This document outlines the comprehensive CRM features planning for SwedPrime, a white-label SaaS platform for Swedish cleaning companies. The CRM system is designed to handle customer relationship management with Swedish market compliance (RUT/ROT, GDPR) and multi-tenant architecture.

**Date**: July 27, 2025  
**Version**: 1.0  
**Status**: Planning Phase  
**Target Launch**: CRM MVP Ready for Production

---

## 🎯 **CRM Core Objectives**

### **Primary Goals:**
1. **Customer Management**: Complete customer lifecycle from lead to loyal customer
2. **Swedish Compliance**: RUT/ROT eligibility tracking and GDPR compliance
3. **Multi-tenant Security**: Company data isolation and role-based access
4. **Business Intelligence**: Analytics and reporting for business growth
5. **Operational Efficiency**: Streamlined workflows and automation

### **Success Metrics:**
- Customer acquisition cost reduction by 30%
- Customer retention rate improvement by 25%
- Response time to customer inquiries under 2 hours
- 100% GDPR compliance audit score
- 95% RUT/ROT eligibility accuracy
